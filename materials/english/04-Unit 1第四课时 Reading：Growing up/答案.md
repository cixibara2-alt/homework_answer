### C2（开放题，结合文中细节，答案不唯一，给出两个参考示例）

1. Goodall was **curious about nature** because **as a little girl she hid in a henhouse for hours just to see how a hen laid an egg, which shows she was curious about animals from a very young age**.

2. Goodall was also **patient and persistent** because **she sat quietly in the forest day after day until a chimpanzee finally allowed her to observe him, and she kept doing careful research for years to understand chimpanzees' lives**.

## 03

### C3（开放题，参考答案）

1. What does "dream" refer to?
**It refers to Jane Goodall's childhood dream of going to Africa to study animals, which she had already decided on by the age of ten and later achieved by travelling to Gombe, Tanzania to study chimpanzees.**

2. What does "forest" symbolize?
**The "forest" symbolizes how her one small personal dream grew into something much bigger that reached far beyond herself — from her research in the Gombe forest, to helping people understand animals better, to the Roots & Shoots youth movement that has now spread to about 87 countries. Just like a single seed can grow into a whole forest, her one dream grew into a worldwide influence, which matches the title "When One Dream Grew into a Forest".**
