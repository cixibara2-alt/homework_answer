**短文（Task 2）参考范文：**

**My Unforgettable Folk Culture Experience**

Last year, during the Dragon Boat Festival, I watched a dragon boat race by the river in my hometown with my family. The drums beat loudly, and the rowers shouted together as their boats flew across the water. Along the riverside, an old man patiently taught me how to wrap a zongzi with bamboo leaves and sticky rice. Everyone around us cheered and laughed, sharing snacks and stories. That warm atmosphere made me feel deeply connected to the people around me. I learned that folk traditions are not just old customs, but a way to bring people's hearts together, and they are truly worth passing down to the next generation.

（约 115 词，按 Opening-Body-Closing 结构写了一次端午节看龙舟、学包粽子的经历，用了具体场景细节来体现民俗的美和精神，仅供参考，写自己真实的经历更好。）
