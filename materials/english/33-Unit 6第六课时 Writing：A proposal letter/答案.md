这份是英语 Unit 6 第六课时 Writing：A proposal letter，共 3 页。
第 1 页开头是"only bad for our study..."，说明这封建议信最开头（问候、自我介绍、提出问题那部分）在前一页，扫描件里没有，下面的 Task 1 里"Causes"两空是根据后文"建议"倒推出来的，不是原文直接写的，已经标注清楚。

## 01

### Task 1（表格填空，每空不超过两个词）

1. **Noise**（问题：学校里的噪音）
2. **shout and run**（原因：学生在走廊里大喊大叫、乱跑——这一空前一页缺失，是根据后面"remind students not to shout or run"这条建议倒推出来的，不是 100% 确定原文用词，仅供参考）
3. **Loud music**（原因：体育场附近放很吵的音乐——同样是根据后面"softer music can be played during breaks"这条建议倒推的，仅供参考）
4. **study**（影响：对学习不好）
5. **health**（影响：对健康不好）
6. **quiet zones**（建议：设立安静区域）
7. **Clear signs**（建议：张贴清晰的标语）
8. **busy areas**（建议：老师和学生志愿者站在人流密集的地方，比如走廊和楼梯）
9. **softer music**（建议：课间播放轻柔的音乐）
10. **quieter**（希望：学校变得更安静、更好）

### Task 2（找出信里的非正式表达，替换成正式表达，仅列出这一页能看到的部分）

| Informal expressions | Formal expressions |
|---|---|
| I'm (writing to...) | I am |
| cut down on (noise) | reduce |
| What's more | In addition / Furthermore |
| looked at（I hope my ideas will be looked at） | considered / taken into consideration |
| Be well,（结尾） | Yours sincerely, / Best regards, |

## 02

### II（开放性写作，建议信，参考范文，仅供参考，不是唯一答案）

**Dear Principal,**

I am Li Hua, a student in Grade 9. I am writing on behalf of the "Make Our School Cleaner and Greener" campaign to share some ideas about the pollution problems on our campus.

Firstly, I have noticed that a lot of plastic bottles and food wrappers are thrown on the ground near the canteen and playground, which makes our campus look messy. Secondly, some classrooms use paper wastefully, and much of it is never recycled. Thirdly, the water fountains sometimes leak, wasting a great deal of water every day.

I would like to make the following suggestions. First, more recycling bins could be placed around the campus, especially near the canteen, so that students can sort their rubbish easily. Second, the school could organise a "Paper-Saving Week" to remind students to print less and reuse paper whenever possible. Third, the school could ask staff to check and repair the water fountains regularly to avoid wasting water.

I believe that with these small changes, our school can become a cleaner and greener place for everyone. I hope my suggestions will be considered.

**Yours sincerely,**
**Li Hua**

## 03

### Checklist（对照上面的范文自查，供参考）

- ☑ Did you greet the person you were writing to? （Dear Principal,）
- ☑ Did you introduce yourself and describe the problem? （I am Li Hua... plastic bottles / wasted paper / leaking fountains）
- ☑ Did you make suggestions? （recycling bins / Paper-Saving Week / repair fountains）
- ☑ Did you state your purpose of the proposal? （写信目的贯穿全文：为"让校园更干净更绿色"活动提建议）
- ☑ Did you express the expected result and close the letter? （相信这些改变能让校园更干净更绿色 + Yours sincerely, Li Hua）
