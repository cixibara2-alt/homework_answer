## 01

### A1

1. **required us to practise public speaking**（要求我们练习公众演讲）
2. **rehearse her speech**（为演讲排练）

4. **took part in the counselling group activity**（参加咨询小组活动）

### A2

第一段（box：A get nervous　B prepare speeches　C gain confidence　D speak to a crowd　E become interested in）：
1. **D．speak to a crowd**（害怕在很多人面前讲话）
2. **B．prepare speeches**（花几周学习怎么准备演讲稿）
3. **A．got nervous**（比赛中还是会紧张）
4. **C．gain confidence**（参加更多活动后开始变得有信心）
5. **E．became interested in**（有些人甚至对公众演讲产生了兴趣）

第二段（box：A as time went on　B forget parts of　C growing-up moment　D learn from experience　E be used to）：
1. **B．forgot parts of**（第一次上台差点忘词）
2. **A．As time went on**（随着时间推移，慢慢习惯了表演）
3. **D．learn from experience**（她学会了从经验中吸取教训）
4. **C．growing-up moment**（加入话剧社是她一个重要的成长时刻）
5. **E．has been used to**（现在已经习惯了在别人面前表演）

## 02

### B（排序）

正确顺序：**B → D → E → C → A**
（先是"一开始觉得难、常出错"（B）→ "第一周看视频、练习做菜"（D）→ "慢慢变得更自信、尝试新菜谱"（E）→ "一个月后请家人试吃"（C）→ "暑假结束时能做简单饭菜，很自豪"（A），按事情发展的时间顺序排列。）

### C1（开放性写作，参考范文，仅供参考，不是唯一答案）

**A Growing-up Moment**

Last term, I had to give a speech in front of my whole class for the first time. At first, I felt extremely nervous and my hands were shaking. During the speech, I almost forgot my words, but I took a deep breath and continued. Afterwards, my classmates clapped for me, and I felt proud of myself. Since then, I have become more confident when speaking in public. I learned that facing my fears is the first step to growing up.

（约 80 词，涵盖了：成长时刻是什么、发生了什么、当时感受、之后的变化、学到了什么，时间线用了 Last term / At first / During the speech / Afterwards / Since then 串起来。）
