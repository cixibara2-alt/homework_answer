## 01

### A（连线配对）

第一组：1－**C**（restoration 修复工作）；2－**E**（professional 需要专业培训技能的）；3－**A**（equipment 器材、设备）；4－**D**（responsibility 职责）；5－**B**（passion 热情）

第二组：1－**D**（patience 耐心）；2－**A**（damage 损害）；3－**E**（treasure 珍贵的东西）；4－**C**（require 需要）；5－**B**（certification 资格证书）

### B

1. **to repair old books ... over a long period of time**（A book restorer's job is to repair old books which have been damaged over a long period of time.）
2. **was badly damaged**（The old bridge was badly damaged by the storm...）
3. **operating on patients**（The surgeons are busy operating on patients in the city hospital.）
4. **have you worked as**（How long have you worked as a book saver?）
5. **was willing to work overtime**（She was willing to work overtime to finish the project on time.）
6. **is our responsibility**（保持每件设备干净安全是我们的职责）

## 02

### C1（采访记录表填空，均可在采访原文里直接找到对应内容）
