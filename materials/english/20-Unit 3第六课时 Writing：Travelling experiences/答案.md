**短文（B）参考范文：**

**My Memorable Travel Story**

Last summer, I went to Xi'an with my parents during a school trip. We visited the ancient city wall, where music shows and dance performances filled the streets. I was amazed by a group of artists making sugar paintings shaped like animals. I talked with one of the artists, and he kindly showed me how to make the shapes so quickly, though he said it took years of practice. Watching him work made me feel excited and inspired. I learned that behind every beautiful piece of art, there is a lot of patience and hard work. This trip taught me to respect traditional skills, and I will never forget it.

（约 114 词，按 Opening-Body-Closing 结构写了一次去西安的旅行，包含了时间地点人物、旅行中的细节和遇到的人、以及感受和收获，仅供参考，写自己真实的经历更好。）
