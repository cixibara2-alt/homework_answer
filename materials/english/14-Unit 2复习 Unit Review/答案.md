### D（Rosalind Franklin 完形填空）

1. **C．who**（teachers 是人，关系代词用 who）
2. **A．any**（didn't receive any degrees，否定句用 any）
3. **B．up**（didn't give up，没有放弃）
4. **B．to take**（used X-rays to take pictures，目的状语用不定式）
5. **A．which**（shapes 是物，关系代词用 which）
6. **C．walking**（spend time doing sth，花时间做某事）
7. **A．a**（scientist 首次提到，泛指，用 a；scientist 辅音开头不用 an）
8. **C．fairly**（treat women as fairly as men，修饰动词 treat 用副词）
9. **A．as**（the same ... as，和……一样）
10. **A．had to**（had to + 动词原形；needed 后面还需要 to，和原句不符；might 语气不符合"她确实做到了"这个事实）

### E（Anna 采访牙医 Dr Yang 阅读回答）

1. **Anna interviewed her aunt, Dr Yang, who is a dentist.**

2. **Before the interview, Anna thought a dentist's job was quite simple — just checking teeth, fixing small problems, and reminding patients to eat less sugar. She even worried her report would be boring.**

3. **According to Dr Yang, the hardest part of her job is not using professional tools, but helping nervous patients feel safe.**

4. **Dr Yang did not hurry the boy. She first showed him the small mirror, let him hold it, and explained each step in simple words, so trust was built step by step, and the boy eventually opened his mouth and even laughed.**

## 05

5. **Anna came to understand that a professional job is not just about what a person can do, but also about how a person treats others — she began to see dentists not only as people who repair teeth, but also as listeners, teachers and comfort-givers.**

6.（开放题，参考答案，30 词以内，合理的其他写法也可以）
**Don't worry! Dentists like Dr Yang care about your feelings, not just your teeth. They go slowly, explain everything clearly, and make sure you feel safe the whole time.**
