这份是英语 Unit 3 第五课时 Grammar：Infinitives after Wh-words，共 2 页。

## 01

### A.I

1. **what**（不知道要带什么去露营）
2. **how**（怎么正确使用旅行转换插头）
3. **when**（讨论什么时候出发好避开堵车）
4. **how**（记不记得怎么准备急救包）
5. **whether**（讨论要不要穿登山靴）
6. **which**（两条路线不知道选哪条）

### A.II

1. Dad told me **how to use the travel camera during the trip**.
2. Do you know **when to start the trip to Edinburgh**?
3. She is thinking about **where to stay on the first night**.
4. Tom wonders **what to bring for the camping trip**.
5. The guide recommended **which souvenir to buy in Nepal**.
6. In a foreign hospital, he was not sure **who to call**.

### A.III

1. **where to set up the tent**（在哪里搭建帐篷）
2. **what food to prepare**（准备什么食物）
3. **how to use**（如何使用急救包）
4. **when to set off**（什么时候出发）
5. **who to turn to for help**（向谁求助，也可以写 who to ask for help）

## 02

### B Task 1（用"疑问词+不定式"改写划线句）

1. **I didn't know which activity to try first or where to buy real handmade works.**
2. **I asked one of the artists how to make the shapes so quickly.**

### Task 2（回答问题）

1. **Jake went to Xi'an last October, during the National Day holiday.**

2. **He saw music shows and traditional dance performances near the ancient city wall.**

3. **Because a group of artists were making sugar paintings in the shape of animals and flowers, and it was amazing to watch how they turned melted sugar into art within minutes, which caught his attention.**

4. **The beautiful costumes and lights, together with a story that was easy to understand, made it enjoyable for young people to learn about Tang culture through the show.**

5.（开放题，仅供参考）
**I will never forget this trip because I experienced so many unique cultural activities, like watching sugar painting and a Tang-style show, and learned so much about Chinese history in just a few days.**
