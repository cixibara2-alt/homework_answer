### E（阅读回答问题）

1. **Yes, she does.** She says in the first paragraph that after living with her teenage son, she is starting to think that maybe adults also have things to learn from teenagers.

2. **Because adults often want to be good at something right away. If they are not immediately good at it, they tend to give up and tell themselves they didn't really care about it in the first place**, instead of keep trying like her son does.

3. **The son is comfortable with life being unplanned and often changes his plans suddenly** (friends appear, messages come in, and there's suddenly an outing), **while the writer likes everything planned well in advance**, such as lunches and meetings, and prefers to have everything on a calendar.

4. **She usually feels anxious and stressed.** She wants to get to the airport extremely early, keeps watching the clock, imagines problems that haven't happened, and makes backup plans for them.

5. **But if we pay attention, we can learn a lot from teenagers, such as how to accept mistakes calmly, try new things bravely, and enjoy life without worrying about everything in advance.**
（这是开放性续写，只要能呼应前文"父母也能向青少年学习"这个主题，合理的其他写法也可以。）

6.（开放题，个人回答，仅供参考，30 词左右）
**Yes, I am similar to him. I am also comfortable with sudden changes in plans. For example, last weekend my friends suddenly invited me to play basketball, and I happily joined them without any hesitation.**
（如果和文中儿子不太像，也可以写自己作为青少年的某个特质，加一个具体例子说明即可，答案不唯一。）
