## 02

### B.II Task 1（小标题匹配）

1. **A．** Do more than listing events.（对应"不能只是流水账，要包含想法、感受和个人的变化"）
2. **C．** Focus on memorable people.（对应"加入向导或旅伴这样有意思的人物"）
3. **E．** Describe the setting carefully.（对应"写具体的所见所闻所嗅所感，比如街道、天气、食物、建筑"）
4. **D．** Make the story believable.（对应"用事实而不是夸张的描述，诚实的写作让故事可信又有力量"）
5. **B．** Record good ideas quickly.（对应"随时用手机记下好句子和想法"）

### Task 2（词义配对）

1. stand out — **B**（格外突出、显眼）
2. mattered — **C**（很重要、有价值）
3. lively — **A**（充满活力/激情）
4. exaggeration — **D**（夸大其词的描述）
5. trustworthy — **E**（值得信赖的）

## 03

### Task 3（回答问题，参考答案）

1. **You can make travel stories more lively by including interesting characters — instead of only describing the trip, introduce people like a guide or a travel partner and give a few clear details about them.**

2. **Because describing specific things you see, hear, smell and feel (such as the streets, weather, food and buildings) makes the place feel real to readers who have been there, and easy for those who haven't been there to imagine.**

3.（开放题，个人观点，仅供参考）
**I think "Make the story believable" would help me the most, because I sometimes use words like "extremely scary" instead of describing what actually happened. Writing honestly about what I really saw and felt would make my travel stories more trustworthy and convincing.**
